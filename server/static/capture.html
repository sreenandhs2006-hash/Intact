↻

Resumable pieces

The video is sent in pieces from 64 KB to 8 MB. Pieces grow on a good link and shrink on a bad one; after a drop the upload resumes from the last byte the server confirmed.

#

Byte-exact verification

Every piece carries a SHA-256 hash, and so does the whole file. The upload is marked COMPLETE only when the server's hash matches the sender's.

▦

Frame-by-frame check

The server decodes every frame and finds missing, repeated or corrupt frames, with timestamps. The browser's frame log shows whether a gap happened during capture or encoding.
